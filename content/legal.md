### Privacy Policy
This policy explains what personal data is processed when you visit this website, based on the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP / revDSG).

**Controller**  
Mathias Scherer, BlockBite, at the address above. For any privacy question, write to [mathias@blockbite.ch](mailto:mathias@blockbite.ch).

**Hosting**  
This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, browser and the time of the request, to deliver the site and keep it secure. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

**Analytics**  
I use a self-hosted instance of [Umami](https://umami.is), a privacy-focused analytics tool. Umami sets no cookies, does not track you across websites, and only stores aggregated statistics such as page views, referrers, country, and device type. It also records clicks on a few buttons and links (for example "Get in touch") so I can see which parts of the site are useful.

**Contacting me**  
If you write me an email or message, I use the data you send only to answer your request and, if we work together, to handle the project. I don't share it with third parties unless required by law.

**External links**  
This site links to other websites (e.g. GitHub, LinkedIn, X, Telegram, and customer websites). Their own privacy policies apply once you follow such a link.

**Your rights**  
You can ask what personal data I hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted, at any time by emailing [mathias@blockbite.ch](mailto:mathias@blockbite.ch).
